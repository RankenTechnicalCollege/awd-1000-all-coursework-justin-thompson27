History

This well loved card shop opened in the year 2011. Our humble store was founded to create community in St. Louis. Our goal is to bring together all of the TCG player to one place where they can all play together.

What to Do

In our store it is full of tables set up for different popular card games. We have tables for Shadowverse, Magic the Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh, Pokemon, Digimon and Cardfight Vanguard. We sell the newest card packs for all these games and will always have them on the wall on their launch.
